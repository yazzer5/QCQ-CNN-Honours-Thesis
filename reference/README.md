# References

The two papers this work builds on. Both are open access; read them at the publisher.

- **Long, Huang, Ye, Futamura & Sakurai (2025).** Hybrid quantum-classical-quantum convolutional neural networks. *Scientific Reports* 15, 31780. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-13417-1. The QCQ-CNN architecture (frozen random quanvolutional front-end + CNN + QNN head) reproduced here as M1.
- **Hur, Kim & Park (2022).** Quantum convolutional neural network for classical data classification. *Quantum Machine Intelligence* 4, 3. https://doi.org/10.1007/s42484-021-00061-x. Source of the Circuit 4 ansatz used as the trainable front-end (M2, M3, M5).

Other works cited in the write-ups (Schuld, Sweke & Meyer 2021; Berberich et al. 2024;
Bishop 1995; Geirhos et al. 2018; Xu, Evans & Qi 2018; Hendrycks & Dietterich 2019) are
listed with links in `docs/analysis/V15_5_MECHANISM.md` and `V15_5_THEORY.md`.
